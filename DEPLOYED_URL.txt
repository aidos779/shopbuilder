Pre-defense deployment checks:
1. Open FRONTEND_URL.
2. Open API_URL/health and confirm status is ok.
3. Open SWAGGER_URL and confirm Swagger loads.
4. Run the full flow from FRONTEND_FLOW_CHECKLIST.md.
5. Confirm worker logs show email, webhook, abandoned-cart, and subscription workers started.
